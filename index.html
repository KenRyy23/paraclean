ParaClean

San Jose Heights Executive Subdivision · Antipolo City

Download for Android

Version 2.4.1 · about 75 MB · Android 8 or newer

How to install · Paano i-install

  1. Tap Download for Android and wait for it to finish. I-tap ang “Download for Android” at hintaying matapos.
  2. Open the downloaded ParaClean.apk. If asked, allow “Install unknown apps”, then go back. Buksan ang ParaClean.apk. Kapag tinanong, payagan ang “Install unknown apps”.
  3. Tap Install, then Open. Sign up with your Block and Lot. I-tap ang Install, tapos Open. Mag-sign up gamit ang inyong Block at Lot.
iPhone users: this download works on Android phones only. Please ask the HOA office for the iPhone option. · Para sa Android lamang ang download na ito.
Salamat po! · Questions? Contact the SJHES HOA office.
